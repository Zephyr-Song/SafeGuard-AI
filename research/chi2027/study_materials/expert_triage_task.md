# Expert Artifact Rating and Handoff Triage Task

Status: draft for institutional review and expert calibration.

## Part A: blinded artifact rating

Experts receive condition-neutral artifacts in randomized blocks. Product
names, chat labels, interface screenshots, and condition metadata are removed.

For each artifact:

1. complete the rubric in `../90_blinded_artifact_quality_rubric.md`;
2. cite the section supporting each rating;
3. record consequential missing information;
4. record any unsupported approval, compliance, legal, safety, fairness, or
   community claim;
5. guess the condition and confidence only after the rating is locked.

Every artifact receives two independent ratings. Do not replace independent
ratings with consensus.

## Part B: counterbalanced handoff triage

Each expert receives four unresolved-question packets:

- two structured provenance-preserving packets;
- two unstructured protocol/chat packets.

Packet format, case, and order are counterbalanced.

For each packet:

1. start the server timer;
2. assign or redirect the issue to a role;
3. request clarification if needed;
4. write advice and rationale;
5. identify missing evidence;
6. state a closure condition or explain why closure is not currently possible;
7. rate routing appropriateness, evidence sufficiency, advice actionability,
   and provenance confidence from 1 to 5;
8. complete the task.

## Short interview

- What information reduced reconstruction work?
- What information was distracting or insufficient?
- When did the suggested expert role appear wrong?
- Which decisions should never be delegated to SafeBARS?
- Did faster triage ever reduce deliberation quality?

## Interpretation rule

Faster review is not automatically better. A triage-time advantage may be
described as beneficial only if consequential issue coverage and advice quality
do not deteriorate.
