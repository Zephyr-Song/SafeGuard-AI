# Formative Requirements Interview Guide

Status: draft for six approved sessions before design and material freeze.

Target sample:

- three people who recently prepared a sensitive human-facing protocol;
- three people who recently supported or reviewed such protocols.

Duration: 35-45 minutes.

The purpose is to refine design requirements, case realism, rubric wording, and
expert handoff packets. It is not an effectiveness evaluation.

## Opening

1. What kinds of human-facing research, evaluation, UX, service, consultation,
   or co-design protocols have you prepared or reviewed?
2. Please describe the last time you prepared or reviewed an ethics
   application. What information was easy or difficult to obtain?

## Current workflow and breakdowns

3. Where do applicants most often provide generic wording instead of an
   operational procedure?
4. Which parts require repeated clarification?
5. What makes a protocol easy or difficult to review?
6. Which important issues are poorly represented in a static application form?

## AI-era questions

7. If AI is used in or around the research, what additional information must be
   made explicit?
8. Which AI-related questions can be prepared with software, and which require
   institutional or domain authority?
9. What would make LLM assistance misleading or unsafe in this work?

## Handoff

10. When should a system stop and ask for a person?
11. Who might that person be: ethics reviewer, methods advisor, data-protection
    officer, safeguarding lead, community partner, AI-governance expert, or
    someone else?
12. What minimum evidence must a handoff packet contain before you can respond?
13. What would count as a sufficient closure condition?

## Artifact walkthrough

Show one fictional protocol extract and one draft handoff.

14. What is useful, missing, ambiguous, or unnecessarily burdensome?
15. Which wording might create false confidence?
16. What should be added, removed, or made optional?

## Closing

17. What should this system never decide?
18. Who else should be involved in designing or evaluating this workflow?

## Analysis

Use a matrix with:

- current practice;
- recurring breakdown;
- evidence need;
- role and authority;
- non-delegable decision;
- provisional design goal;
- proposed change; and
- unanticipated finding.

Maintain a dated feedback-to-design log. Do not call supervisor or developer
comments participant findings.
