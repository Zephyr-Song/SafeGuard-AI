# SafeBARS Post-Task Questionnaire

Status: wording draft. Freeze and preregister the exact item order, scale
direction, and scoring before data collection.

Use a seven-point response from 1 (strongly disagree) to 7 (strongly agree)
unless otherwise stated. Randomization is not required; keep order identical
across conditions.

## Perceived control

1. I felt in control of the important revisions in my final artifact.
2. I could disagree with or correct the system when needed.
3. I could understand why important suggestions appeared.
4. I could decide which questions should remain unresolved.

## Usefulness for preparation

5. The interface helped me identify consequential weaknesses in the protocol.
6. The interface helped me turn concerns into specific protocol changes.
7. The interface helped me prepare material that a human reviewer could inspect.

## Verification and reliance

8. I checked important suggestions against the supplied protocol.
9. I could distinguish supported suggestions from uncertain suggestions.
10. I would seek qualified human advice before relying on unresolved claims.
11. I may have accepted some suggestions because they sounded confident.

Item 11 is reverse-direction and must not be silently dropped.

## Confidence

12. I am confident that the final artifact clearly describes the proposed
    procedure.
13. I am confident that the final artifact clearly identifies what still needs
    expert review.

## Workload

Administer the approved Raw NASA-TLX presentation for:

- Mental Demand;
- Temporal Demand;
- Performance;
- Effort; and
- Frustration.

Physical Demand may be omitted because the task is screen-based, but this
adaptation must be disclosed. Do not call the custom items above a validated
psychometric scale.

## Open questions

14. What most influenced your revisions?
15. Which suggestion, output, or interaction did you distrust or substantially
    change, and why?
16. What important question remained unresolved?
