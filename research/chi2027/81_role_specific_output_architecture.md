# Role-specific output architecture

## Teacher requirement

SafeBARS must produce:

1. an ethical approval application document and a research design for the standard user; and
2. a summary of standard users' ethics applications and the advice given to them for expert users.

## Implemented standard-user outputs

### Ethics application draft

The application draft is submission-oriented. It organizes the selected generic application profile, field completeness, project context, recruitment, consent, procedures, risk, data management, AI use, researcher decisions, and unresolved expert review. It explicitly requires transfer into the institution's current official form and does not claim approval.

### Research design and ethics-in-practice plan

The research design is operational rather than form-oriented. It covers purpose and methodological rationale, participants and sampling, recruitment, encounter stages, procedures, consent, withdrawal, safeguarding, data generation and analysis, AI oversight, framework-grounded trade-offs, expert dependencies, design readiness, and next actions. Missing material remains visible as a verification task instead of being invented by AI.

## Implemented expert-user outputs

### Per-case expert review summary

An invited expert can export the prioritized handoff queue for one protocol, including exact evidence passages, the AI stopping boundary, researcher position, expert advice and rationale, researcher response, and linked protocol revision.

### Cross-application caseload summary

The expert caseload can export a single Word summary across every invitation stored in the current browser session. The server validates the expert capability token for every included case before producing the document. The summary contains aggregate workload, application completeness and gaps, unresolved priorities, advice records, researcher responses, and linked revisions.

## Boundary

These outputs support preparation and review. They are not institutional approval decisions, an institution-wide case register, or substitutes for required identity, retention, security, and official-form workflows.
